Why First Light

Being quiet isn't the same as being unsure.

Many students understand well but hesitate to share what they think. Marks and report cards don't show this, but it shapes how a student learns, makes friends and grows. We start by understanding why they hold back.

They know, but don't speak

The answer is in their head, but the hand never goes up in class.

Fear of getting it wrong

One-word answers and silence feel safer than the risk of a mistake.

Unsure where they're heading

Too many choices about subjects, courses and careers, and no one to think them through with.

Our approach

Understand first. Then build.

Every program begins with listening. We don't push students to perform; we find out what's holding them back and work from there.

  1. 01

    Understand

    A friendly, counselling-style conversation and simple self-assessments to learn how the student thinks and feels.

  2. 02

    Build confidence

    Creative, low-pressure activities that let them practise expressing ideas in a safe space.

  3. 03

    Express freely

    Clear feedback and practical next steps for students and parents, so progress continues at home and at school.

Find Your Voice

Understand • Build confidence • Express freely

A one-to-one personal discovery and confidence session for children aged 5–12. We help your child discover that there is no "perfect" way to speak. There is only their way.

What happens in the session

  1. 1

    Get to know

    A friendly conversation so your child feels comfortable and builds trust.

  2. 2

    Self-assessment

    Fun, simple worksheets (rating scales and personal questions) that show us how your child feels.

  3. 3

    Thinking activities

    Creative, open-ended activities to explore thinking style and communication strengths.

  4. 4

    Communication observation

    We observe speaking style, hesitation patterns, confidence levels and comfort areas.

  5. 5

    Feedback & guidance

    We share our insights with parents and suggest ways to support your child's growth.

What we understand

  • Why your child hesitates to speak
  • What is stopping them from expressing themselves
  • Confidence level in different situations
  • Thinking style & idea generation
  • Communication strengths & areas to improve
  • How we can help them speak freely

Who it's for

Children aged 5–12 who:

  • Give one-word answers
  • Hesitate while speaking
  • Fear making mistakes
  • Find it hard to express ideas
  • Need a confidence boost

What you get

  • One-to-one session
  • Assessment worksheets
  • Detailed feedback report for parents
  • Personalised insights & observations
  • Practical suggestions for next steps

Before the session

  • Please don't share the assessment sheets with your child before the session.
  • If you have a printer, print the sheets we send when we ask you to.
  • If not, your child can complete them online during the session.
  • This is a one-to-one session. Please make sure your child has a quiet space.

About us

The people behind First Light

We started First Light because we've seen how many capable students go unheard. Our goal is simple: help every student find the confidence to share what they know.

Neha Kiran

Founder

Biology, IISER Tirupati · English communication tutor

Neha trained in Biology at IISER Tirupati and did research at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. Neha now works as an English communication tutor at Shloka – A Birla Open Minds International School, helping students build their language and speaking skills, and teaches life sciences through concept-based learning. A former school pupil leader and Carnatic vocalist, Neha has led science demonstrations and volunteered to teach underprivileged students. Neha speaks English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil.

Vigneswaran Ravi

Co-founder

PhD researcher in Physics, University of Cambridge

A Harding Distinguished Postgraduate Scholar at the Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge. Vigneswaran's interest in science began with a simple curiosity at school and grew, one question at a time, into research on new materials for flexible, sustainable electronics. At First Light, Vigneswaran brings that same belief: every student's curiosity deserves the confidence to be heard.
